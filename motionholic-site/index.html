Posting Isn’t The Problem

You didn't start posting to become a video editor. You started because you needed more clients. So you film on Sunday, edit for three nights, publish, and wait. A few likes come in. No enquiries. Meanwhile the work that actually pays you sat untouched all week.

The issue usually isn't your message. It's that the edit never leads anyone anywhere. Nothing in the first seconds to stop the person who could afford you. No structure that builds enough trust to make them curious. No clear reason to book.

That's the part we fix. Since 2021 we've cut over 1,200 videos for coaches, creators and brands, and every one is built around a single question: will this make someone want to work with you? You get your evenings back, and your content starts doing a job.
