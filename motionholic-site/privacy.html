Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit motionholic.com or get in touch. Motionholic is run by Ayon Kumar Paul Ovi.

What this website collects

This site does not run analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Simply reading these pages does not create a profile of you.

What you send us

If you email us or book a call, we receive whatever you choose to share, typically your name, email address and details about your project. We use it to reply to you and to do the work you have asked for. We do not sell it or pass it to anyone for marketing.

Services we rely on

  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and reliability.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of the site and may set a security cookie to tell real visitors from bots.
  • Calendly handles call bookings. Anything you enter on the booking form is processed by Calendly under its own privacy policy.
  • YouTube hosts the videos shown here. Thumbnails load as images, and nothing is requested from YouTube's player until you click play. Once you do, YouTube may set cookies under its own policy.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site.

How long we keep things

We keep project emails and files for as long as we are working together, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back. Ask us to delete your information at any time and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email ayonpalovi10@gmail.com and we will sort it out.

Contact

Questions about any of this go to ayonpalovi10@gmail.com.
